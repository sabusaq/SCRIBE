4. How the risk score is computed

Score (0-100) = distinct exact-match indicators (25 for the first, +12 each after, capped 80) + up to 12 for hit volume (1 per 5 hits) + up to 8 for distinct substring indicators (2 each) + up to 25 for Sigma critical/high detections (1 per 2) + 10 if matches span 3+ artifact types (5 for 2). Bands: 70+ CRITICAL, 45+ HIGH, 25+ MEDIUM, 10+ LOW, else MINIMAL. Multiple distinct exact-match indicators corroborating each other drive the score hardest, since that is the strongest available evidence of a real compromise. The score weighs evidence volume and corroboration; it does not assess intent or impact, and a MINIMAL score on a host with blind spots is not a clean bill.

Generated by SCRIBE on {{DATE}}. Findings are mechanical results from the parsed artifacts, the supplied IOC set, and Sigma rules. Hosts with failed or thin coverage are blind spots, not clean results.
